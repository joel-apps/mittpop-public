What Mitt Pop is

Mitt Pop measures the time between two video frames you mark, and estimates the pitch speed from that time and the distance you choose. Results depend on the frames marked, the distance, the release and catch points, the video’s frame rate and lighting. A radar gun can read differently.

Pitch counter and age comparison

The pitch counter summarizes published rules and guidelines (Little League®, MLB/USA Baseball Pitch Smart) as of the date shown in the app, or limits you enter. Rules change and differ by league, tournament and state; league officials decide who may pitch. The age comparison uses averages from a published study and is a rough guide only.
