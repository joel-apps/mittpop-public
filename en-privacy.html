Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what the Mitt Pop iPhone app does with your data. Mitt Pop is published by Joel Hernandez (“we”). Contact: joelhcode@gmail.com.

In short: your videos stay on your iPhone, and your players and readings stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. No accounts, no ads, no usage analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

Videos

Videos you record in the app, videos you pick from Photos, and the short clips kept with saved pitches are stored on your iPhone. Share clips (the slow-motion video with the speed on it) are made on your iPhone and only leave it if you choose to share them. Videos are never uploaded to us or anyone else.

Camera and microphone

The camera is used only to film pitches when you tap record. Mitt Pop does not use the microphone and records no sound.

Photos

When you choose a slow-motion video, iOS asks for photo library access so the app can read that video’s original file; Mitt Pop only opens the video you pick. If you choose “Save Video” when sharing a clip, iOS asks for permission to add it to your Photos.

Players, readings and pitch counts

Player names or nicknames you type, the readings you save and your pitch-counter outings are stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud (Apple’s key-value storage, tied to your Apple ID) so they appear on your other devices; we cannot access it. Video files are not stored in iCloud.

Age range (where the law requires it)

On iOS 26.2 and later, in places where laws require apps to check age (such as Texas), the app asks Apple once for your age range. The answer is not stored or shared; the app keeps only the date the check ran. Apple handles parental consent for downloads and purchases.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether Pro is unlocked. RevenueCat receives your App Store transaction information and an anonymous app user identifier, and uses them to give us aggregate sales reports.

Notifications

If you start a free trial, the app can schedule one local reminder on your iPhone two days before the trial ends. It is created on your device; nothing is sent to us.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t ask for your name, email or any account.
  • We don’t collect usage analytics (what you do in the app).
  • We don’t show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • We don’t sell your data or share it except with the services named on this page.

Retention and your choices

Deleting the app removes its data and videos from your iPhone. The copy of your players, readings and pitch counts in your iCloud stays there and comes back if you reinstall; delete players in the app first to remove them everywhere. Because we receive none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete, but you can email us with any question.

Children and teens

Mitt Pop is made for parents and coaches, and teens may use it too. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children: everything stays on the device and in the user’s own iCloud.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
