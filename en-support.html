Mitt Pop

Pitch speed from slow-motion video, for youth baseball and softball. Film a pitch at 240 fps, mark the release and the catch frame by frame, and get the speed. No radar gun needed.

Common questions

How do I film a pitch?

Hold the phone sideways, from the side of the pitch, about 35–40 ft away, with the pitcher and the catcher both in view. Daylight works best: iPhones film fewer frames in low light.

Which frames do I mark?

Release: the first frame where the ball has left the hand. Catch: the first frame where the ball touches the mitt.

How accurate is it?

At 240 fps each frame is about 4 milliseconds, so one frame off changes the reading by well under 1 mph. The bigger source of error is distance: each foot of difference changes the speed by about 2–3%. Mitt Pop uses the real time of every frame, subtracts where the ball is released and adds where it is caught (you can adjust both under Setup), and corrects for the ball slowing down in the air, so the number compares with how a radar gun reads. It is still an estimate from video, not a radar.

Why is the speed higher than distance ÷ time?

A radar gun shows the speed as the ball leaves the hand. The ball slows down on the way, so the average over the flight is a few percent lower. Mitt Pop shows both.

I picked a video and it says it can’t be timed.

The video arrived at 30 fps, which usually means it lost its slow motion (for example after being sent through some apps). Use the original slow-motion video, or record in Mitt Pop.

Why does iOS ask for access to all my photos?

To read the original slow-motion file of the video you pick, iOS needs photo library access. Mitt Pop only opens the video you choose. You can avoid this completely by recording inside the app.

Is the pitch counter official?

No. It summarizes published rules (Little League® Baseball and Softball, MLB/USA Baseball Pitch Smart) or limits you enter. Your league, tournament or state may differ: confirm with your league officials. It is not medical advice.

What does Pro include?

Measuring is always free. Pro adds more than one player, every saved pitch with the full history, the comparison with averages for the player’s age, share clips without the Mitt Pop mark, and a CSV export. Pro is a yearly subscription with a free trial, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

How do I restore or cancel?

Restore: open Players → Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID. Cancel a subscription in your iPhone’s Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel it.
